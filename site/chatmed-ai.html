01 / PRODUCT JOURNEY

Six stages. One connected experience.

A high-level view of the journey reflected by the supplied application snapshot. Eesha's contribution is called out separately in each stage.

01

Understand the healthcare context

Clarify the user problem, intended health-information experience, and the needs that a conversational healthcare product aims to address.

Eesha's part · Build product context and contribute a neuro-electrophysiology perspective to medical-information discussions.
02

Shape the product experience

Bring together medical AI chat, voice interaction, medical tools, health-oriented pages, and supporting account and documentation journeys.

Eesha's part · Help review medical terminology, context, and the clarity of healthcare information relevant to her scope.
03

Build and integrate the application

The supplied frontend uses React, TypeScript, routed product modules, authentication, API services, localisation, voice streaming, and product workflows.

Eesha's part · Support assigned Python-based backend/API integration tasks.
04

Validate medical information and AI outputs

Review health-related content and AI-generated responses for clarity, relevance, and issues that should be flagged for product review.

Eesha's part · Medical information validation and AI output evaluation.
05

Beta test real user journeys

Exercise the application flows, report friction and unexpected behaviour, and provide feedback for refinement.

Eesha's part · Participate in beta testing and share structured feedback.
06

Iterate with cross-functional feedback

Use testing findings and review feedback to help guide fixes, improvements, and follow-up validation.

Eesha's part · Re-test relevant flows and contribute to the feedback and integration loop.

Documentation, API information, dashboard, pricing, payment routes, blog, and support pages.
